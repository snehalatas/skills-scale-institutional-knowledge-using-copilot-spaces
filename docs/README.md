# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This folder contains OctoAcme's shared project management guidance: a lightweight, customer-first and iterative approach to initiating, planning, delivering, releasing, and improving projects.

## Project management processes summary

OctoAcme's project lifecycle starts by validating the business need, aligning stakeholders, and recording the goal, success measures, timeline, risks, and resource needs in a project one-pager. Once the outcome, priority, and team availability are clear, the team plans delivery by prioritizing and estimating backlog items, defining acceptance criteria and the Definition of Done, setting milestones, and identifying dependencies. Work is delivered in small increments, tracked on a project board, and reviewed at sprint or milestone demos before release and closeout.

The Project Manager coordinates delivery, schedules, risks, dependencies, and communications; the Product Manager/Lead owns product outcomes, backlog priorities, and success measures. Developers estimate, build, test, and review solutions; QA validates acceptance and quality; stakeholders provide input and approvals. Regular coordination includes team standups, weekly PM/Product and delivery syncs, demos, stakeholder updates, and timely escalation of blockers. Project status and risks are kept visible in a shared source of truth.

Quality is planned and checked throughout delivery through clear acceptance criteria and a Definition of Done, reviewed pull requests, automated tests and linting, unit and applicable integration tests, security scans, and manual QA where needed. Release readiness includes meeting acceptance criteria and CI checks, preparing release notes and rollback plans, staging smoke tests, and post-deployment verification. Retrospectives after sprints, releases, milestones, or incidents convert lessons into a small number of owned and measurable improvement actions.
